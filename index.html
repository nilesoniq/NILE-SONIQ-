Discover Artists on NileSoniq

Explore artists from Uganda and discover the songs they have submitted to NileSoniq. Each public artist profile is built around the artist's own information and approved music, so listeners can learn who created a release as well as listen to it.

Artist profiles may include genre, musical style, years active, website and social links where the artist has provided them. Public music submissions are reviewed before they are displayed.

If you are an artist, you can create a profile, submit original music, provide accurate release information, and use NileSoniq to connect with listeners.

NileSoniq Songs

Discover approved music from NileSoniq artists.

Browse Original Music

NileSoniq is a place to discover music submitted by artists and approved for public listening. Browse releases, listen to songs, view artist information, and learn more about each track through its available description and release details.

Song pages can include information such as genre, release date, producer, featured artists, record label, location, and the artist's own description. Play counts are used to help listeners discover popular music through the site's charts.

Music submitted by artists is reviewed before it becomes publicly available. If you believe a published song infringes your copyright or violates our rules, you can report it or use the DMCA process.

NileSoniq News

Original music stories, artist features, release updates, interviews, community stories, and reporting about the Ugandan music scene.

The NileSoniq Newsroom

The NileSoniq newsroom is intended for original editorial content about music and the people who make it. Articles may cover artist spotlights, interviews, new releases, music events, community stories, and developments in Uganda's music scene.

News articles are written and published by authorized NileSoniq administrators. We do not promise that every article will cover every event, and we do not publish placeholder stories simply to increase the number of pages on the site.

When available, an article includes a publication date, a summary, and the full story so readers can understand the subject without leaving NileSoniq.

About NileSoniq

Learn more about NileSoniq and our mission.

Welcome to NileSoniq - An Artist-First Ugandan Music Platform

NileSoniq is a music streaming platform built in Kampala, Uganda, and launched in 2026. Our mission is to give Ugandan artists a place to share original music, provide useful release information, and connect with listeners in Uganda and beyond.

Who We Are

We are a team of Ugandan music lovers, developers, and artists based in Kampala. We built NileSoniq to help local artists share their music and reach more listeners.

Our Platform

NileSoniq hosts original music uploaded directly by artists who own the rights to their work.

What Makes Us Different

Artist-First: Artists upload their music and keep ownership.

Fair Play Counting: A play is counted after 30 seconds of listening.

No Piracy: Uploaded content is reviewed and approved.

Supporting Local: We focus on promoting Ugandan talent.

Our Vision

To become a leading platform for original African music where artists can reach fans and build their audiences.
