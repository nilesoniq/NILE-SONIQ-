About NileSoniq

Learn more about NileSoniq and our mission.

Welcome to NileSoniq - An Artist-First Ugandan Music Platform

NileSoniq is a music streaming platform built in Kampala, Uganda, and launched in 2026. Our mission is to give Ugandan artists a place to share original music, provide useful release information, and connect with listeners in Uganda and beyond.

Who We Are

We are a team of Ugandan music lovers, developers, and artists based in Kampala. We built NileSoniq to help local artists share their music and reach more listeners.

Our Platform

NileSoniq hosts original music uploaded directly by artists who own the rights to their work.

What Makes Us Different

Artist-First: Artists upload their music and keep ownership.

Fair Play Counting: A play is counted after 30 seconds of listening.

No Piracy: Uploaded content is reviewed and approved.

Supporting Local: We focus on promoting Ugandan talent.

Our Vision

To become a leading platform for original African music where artists can reach fans and build their audiences.
